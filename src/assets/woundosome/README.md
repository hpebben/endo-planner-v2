# Foot angiogram references

The AP and lateral PNGs are panel extractions from Figure 3B and Figure 3A,
respectively, in Patrone et al., *The “Woundosome” Concept and Its Impact on
Procedural Outcomes in Patients With Chronic Limb-Threatening Ischemia*,
Journal of Endovascular Therapy (2024), DOI: 10.1177/15266028241231745.

Source supplied for this feature:
https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/322037/1/L.Patrone%20Journal%20of%20Endovascular%20Therapy%20%282024%29%201-2.pdf

The images show the published reference left foot after revascularization. They
are educational reference images, not the user's case imaging and not a normal
arterial atlas. Colored markers and approximate projected wound locations are
separate SVG overlays. They do not indicate proven perfusion territories,
vessel patency, or sharp angiosome boundaries. Dorsal/plantar locations overlap
in the AP projection; medial/lateral heel locations overlap in the lateral
projection. The heel is assessed on the lateral image because the AP reference
field does not adequately show the heel branches.
